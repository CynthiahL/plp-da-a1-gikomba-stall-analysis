### Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce retail stall at the bustling Gikomba Market, but she lacks structured visibility into her financial trajectory and supply chain stability. For the past 14 days, she has manually tracked her daily sales revenue and maintained a rough inventory ledger in a notebook. Without analytical tools, she cannot definitively tell if her business is growing week-on-week, which products yield the strongest profit margins, how to optimize her weekday versus weekend stock levels, or which critical goods are at imminent risk of stockouts. This analysis translates her raw ledger into clear, actionable baseline metrics using pure Python to optimize her restocking schedule and maximize revenue.

### Top Three Findings
1. **Strong Revenue Expansion:** Wanjiku's business is on an upward growth trajectory, posting a **10.7% week-on-week revenue increase** as total weekly sales climbed from KES 22,050.00 in Week 1 to KES 24,400.00 in Week 2.
2. **Extreme Weekend Spikes:** Customer demand heavily skews toward the weekend, with Saturday and Sunday sales averaging **KES 4,900.00**, which is an **82.5% performance jump** over the weekday average of KES 2,685.00. 
3. **Critical Stock Depletion:** Both **Tomatoes and Sukuma** have completely depleted past their safety reorder thresholds (18kg and 9kg remaining, respectively), threatening a total stockout of Sukuma which yields an excellent **40.0% gross profit margin**.
